Data sources & licences

Hanabi doesn't invent Japanese. Every kanji, reading, meaning, word and sentence comes from these community datasets, used under their licences.

KANJIDIC2 · JMdict

© Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), used under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. Converted to JSON by the jmdict-simplified project. KANJIDIC2 edition {{ meta.kanjidic2_date }}, JMdict edition {{ meta.jmdict_date }}.

The Jōyō list and school grades in KANJIDIC2 follow the official 2010 Jōyō table and the 2020 elementary-school grade allocation by Japan's Ministry of Education (MEXT). They're a community dataset, not an official government publication.

Dictionary

The Dictionary page searches the complete JMdict ({{ '{:,}'.format(meta.dict_entries|int) if meta.dict_entries else '' }} entries, edition {{ meta.jmdict_date }}), © EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0. Example sentences are from Tatoeba. Dictionaries you import yourself stay private to your account; their licences are up to their authors.

Anki

Hanabi can read and write Anki deck packages (.apkg). Anki is a separate free program by Ankitects; Hanabi isn't affiliated with it.
