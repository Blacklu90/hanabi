What gets synced

Cards and their review history, decks, imported Anki decks (with images and audio), your notes, your dictionaries, lessons, quizzes, XP and achievements. When a card was studied on both devices, the most recent review wins. Nothing is ever deleted by a sync.

Not synced: your password and profile, and display settings (theme, colours), which stay per device. On a computer, your firewall may ask once whether Shuinkan can use the network — allow it for private networks.
